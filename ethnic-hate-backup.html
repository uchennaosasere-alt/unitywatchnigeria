Contribute responsibly

Help Document an Incident

If you have information about a possible incident of ethnic hate or discrimination, you can submit a report for review. Clear details and supporting evidence can help reviewers understand what happened.

  • Describe what happened as clearly as possible.
  • Include the date and location, when known.
  • Provide relevant supporting photos, videos or documents.
  • Distinguish what you personally witnessed from what you heard.
  • Avoid publishing private information unnecessarily.

Our principles

Evidence Before Assumptions

Responsible documentation requires fairness, context and respect for the people involved. Reports should never become a reason to target an entire ethnic group.

01

Verify Information

Check dates, context and available evidence before presenting an allegation as established fact.

02

Protect Dignity

Avoid unnecessary exposure of private information and do not encourage harassment or retaliation.

03

Reject Collective Blame

Focus on specific conduct and available evidence, not accusations against an entire ethnic community.
