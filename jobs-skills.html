Understand the verification badges

Every status must have a defined meaning so visitors can understand what has, and has not, been checked.

  • Verified Required checks have been completed and documented, including confirmation of the vacancy with an appropriate employer contact.
  • Pending Verification The listing has been received, but the required checks are incomplete.
  • Verification Failed The listing did not meet the published verification requirements. The reason should be documented.
  • Verification Expired The previous check is no longer current, or the vacancy needs to be reconfirmed.

Phase 02

Responsible Employers & Workplace Standards

Responsible employment goes beyond offering a job. It includes dignity, fair treatment, clear communication, and a willingness to address workplace concerns professionally.

Company registration

Organisations can provide their details and indicate their commitment to responsible workplace practices.

Our Commitment to Responsible Employment

We pledge to treat employees, applicants, interns, and other workers with dignity, fairness, and respect.

We commit to promoting a workplace free from discrimination, harassment, intimidation, and unlawful retaliation. We will communicate employment terms clearly, respect applicable labour laws, and make reasonable efforts to maintain safe and professional working conditions.

We will provide appropriate channels for workplace concerns and make reasonable efforts to address them fairly and professionally.

This form is a demonstration only. It does not register the company, store the pledge, or award any employer status.

Employer participation stages

Each stage should have clear requirements and documented decisions before a company receives a public status.

  • Registration Submitted Company information has been provided for review.
  • Identity Under Review The organisation's identity and representative are being checked.
  • Pledge Accepted An authorised representative has accepted the published workplace pledge.
  • Participating Employer The organisation has met the published participation requirements. This is not a guarantee of future conduct.
  • Under Review or Suspended Participation may be reviewed or suspended in accordance with published rules and a fair process.
A company's registration, pledge, and participation status should be displayed separately from the verification status of its individual job listings.
Our Principles

What responsible employment should look like

These principles provide a starting point for encouraging professional relationships between employers and workers.

Respect & Dignity

Workers should be treated professionally, without humiliation, intimidation, harassment, or abuse.

Fair Opportunity

Recruitment and workplace decisions should be transparent, relevant to the role, and free from unlawful discrimination.

Clear Employment Terms

Workers should receive clear information about their roles, responsibilities, remuneration, and working arrangements.

Safe Working Conditions

Employers should take reasonable steps to provide safe and appropriate working environments.

Accountability

Workplace concerns should be considered fairly, with suitable opportunities to respond and correct mistakes.

Privacy & Confidentiality

Personal information and workplace reports should be handled carefully and shared only when there is an appropriate basis.

Phase 03

Workplace Accountability & Fair Review

People should have a clear way to raise concerns about recruitment and workplace conduct. Every concern deserves appropriate consideration, but an allegation must not automatically be treated as a proven finding.

  1. Concern Received

    The concern is submitted through a designated channel, with only the information reasonably necessary for assessment.

  2. Assessment & Response

    Relevant information is reviewed, and an appropriate opportunity to respond may be provided where necessary.

  3. Outcome & Review

    The matter is handled according to published procedures, with appropriate privacy protections and a route to request review where available.

Types of workplace concerns

Unpaid wages or disputed payments • Bullying and harassment • Discrimination • Misleading recruitment adverts • Unclear employment terms • Unsafe working conditions • Unprofessional recruitment practices.

Fairness notice: This page does not currently receive or investigate complaints. Before launching a reporting service, Unity Watch Nigeria should establish secure handling procedures, evidence standards, privacy safeguards, response opportunities, and an appeal or review process.

Creative Development Programme

Music Production & Creative Development

Supporting emerging artists and independent creatives through practical music education, recording opportunities, and foundational audio skills.

Accessible Creative Learning

Develop your sound. Build your skills.

We aim to create pathways for aspiring musicians, songwriters, vocalists, and independent artists to learn, practise, and develop their creative potential.

  • Music production and beat-making fundamentals.
  • Song arrangement and creative development.
  • Vocal recording and recording techniques.
  • Basic mixing and vocal processing.
  • Access to recording sessions at a subsidised rate, subject to programme availability and confirmed terms.

The aim is to make practical creative development more accessible without compromising professionalism or quality.
