Our Policy

Our principles guide our commitment to fairness, responsible reporting, human dignity and peaceful coexistence throughout Nigeria.

Our Guiding Principles

1. Respect and Equality

We promote respect, dignity and equal treatment for all Nigerians, regardless of ethnic, religious or cultural background.

2. Zero Tolerance for Hate and Discrimination

We oppose ethnic hatred, discrimination, threats and the promotion of violence. Reports should focus on specific incidents and available evidence.

3. Responsible Reporting

Reports should be truthful and submitted in good faith. Deliberately false reports, harassment and attempts to target innocent people are unacceptable.

4. Privacy and Confidentiality

Personal information must be handled carefully and shared only when necessary for a legitimate purpose. We aim to protect the privacy of people submitting reports and those named in them.

5. Fairness and Verification

A report is an allegation, not proof of wrongdoing. Information should be reviewed fairly before conclusions are reached or details are made public.

6. Peaceful Coexistence

We encourage dialogue, mutual understanding and peaceful solutions to disagreements. Our goal is to strengthen relationships across communities, not to create further division.
