Fairness and accountability

Appeals & Review

People and organisations should have a clear way to challenge decisions and request a fair review.

Your right to request a review

You may request a review if you believe a report was rejected unfairly, a published record contains disputed information, or a decision needs further consideration. Submitting an appeal does not guarantee that the original decision will change.

Appeal a Rejected Report

Explain why you believe your report should be reconsidered and provide relevant additional information.

Challenge a Published Record

Identify the disputed record and explain which information you believe is inaccurate or unfairly presented.

Request Another Review

Request reconsideration of a report's status or handling and explain the reason for your request.

Appeal Submission Form

Provide the reference number if one has been assigned to the original report.

Demonstration only. Appeals are not delivered to a review team or saved. A secure reporting system must be connected before real submissions can be received.

Responsible reporting

Privacy, Fairness & Accountability

Every report deserves fair consideration

A submitted allegation is not proof of wrongdoing. Decisions should be based on a fair review of the available information. Where practicable, an appeal should be assessed by someone other than the original decision-maker.


Personal information should be protected, and only appropriate information should be made public. Records and appeals should not be used to threaten, harass, retaliate against or expose private details about another person.
