About Unity Watch Nigeria

Building a Nigeria where differences are respected, communities are protected, and everyone can live with dignity, fairness and peace.

Who We Are

Our Purpose

Unity Watch Nigeria is a platform dedicated to promoting understanding, respect and peaceful coexistence among people of different ethnic, religious and cultural backgrounds across Nigeria.

Our Vision

We envision a Nigeria where every person is treated with dignity, differences are respected, and communities can live together in peace and unity.

Our Mission

Our mission is to raise awareness about ethnic hatred and discrimination, encourage responsible reporting of harmful incidents, and promote fairness, respect and understanding throughout Nigeria.

Our Commitment

We believe that every report deserves careful consideration, every person deserves fair treatment, and every community has a role to play in building a safer and more united Nigeria.
