Responsible Reporting

Help Build a More Accountable Society

Credible documentation can help the public understand incidents involving religiously motivated threats or violence. Reports should be factual, relevant, and submitted responsibly.

If you have information about a concern, use the reporting page to find the available reporting options. Do not put yourself in danger to obtain evidence.

Go to Reporting Page

Before Submitting Information

  • Provide only information you believe to be accurate and relevant.
  • Clearly distinguish what you personally witnessed from what you heard from others.
  • Include dates and locations where safe and appropriate.
  • Do not publish private addresses, personal phone numbers, or other sensitive details.
  • Do not submit threats, fabricated evidence, or material intended to incite hatred.
  • Allow allegations to be reviewed fairly before presenting them as established facts.
