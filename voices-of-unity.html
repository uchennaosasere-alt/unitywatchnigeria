The Good We Share

Recognising the Good in Our Communities

Across Nigeria, people from different backgrounds are making positive contributions to society. From religious leaders who preach peace to teachers who inspire students, and ordinary citizens who demonstrate honesty and kindness, positive change begins with people choosing to do good.

Faith That Builds Bridges

Leadership That Inspires Peace

We recognise constructive contributions from pastors, priests, imams, traditional leaders, and people of different beliefs who encourage compassion, honesty, peaceful coexistence, and respect for human dignity.

Our focus is not on titles, religious affiliation, or popularity. We are interested in positive messages, meaningful actions, and contributions that help people live together in peace.

Character in Action

Honesty, Kindness and Service

Goodness is not limited to people in positions of influence. It can be found in students who stand for honesty, teachers who support their learners, workers who act with integrity, neighbours who show kindness, and citizens who help others without expecting a reward.

We aim to highlight credible examples of integrity, compassion, courage, responsibility, and service to the community.

Recognition Is Not a Verdict: Being featured on Unity Watch Nigeria does not mean that someone is better than everyone else. Equally, a person's absence from our platform must never be taken as evidence that they are dishonest, unkind, or a bad person.

Many people do good things quietly and may never receive public recognition. Our stories represent selected, constructive contributions supported by appropriate information. They are not a complete list of every good person in Nigeria, nor are they a declaration that anyone is perfect.

We highlight positive contributions to inspire others, not to judge the worth of individuals.
